About Me

I'm Manar Hassan, a UI/UX Designer passionate about turning complex ideas into simple, intuitive experiences. My approach blends user research, rapid prototyping, and thoughtful visual design to craft products that feel effortless and delightful. I thrive in collaborative environments where insights from real users guide every decision.

Whether it's defining user flows, creating wireframes, or polishing high-fidelity interfaces, I aim to ship designs that are accessible, scalable, and beautiful.
